# Runbook: fresh Bouchet root, 5-year validation, 35-year projections

Package w (`tcsmewn_v09_20261005w.tar.gz`): package v with a concentration
within rounding distance below zero counted as zero in the ionic strength
(`ROUNDOFF_NEGATIVE_2026-10-05.md`). Section 0w reruns the run that stopped
on it; section 0v is v's procedure, which w follows otherwise.

Package v (`tcsmewn_v09_20261004v.tar.gz`): package u with the particle
histories kept daily, two-hour SSP 2-D checkpoints and 256 G 35-year 2-D
requests (`PARTICLE_RECORDS_DAILY_2026-10-04.md`); every computed value is
u's. Section 0v is the procedure for this root; section 0u is u's record.

Package u (`tcsmewn_v09_20261002u.tar.gz`): package t with the soil-water
paste of the bulk-pH operator open to the laboratory air
(`PASTE_OPEN_TO_AIR_2026-10-02.md`), the campaign's Appelo alpha at 3.4 in
place of 1.3, the uncertainty ensemble's alpha at 1.3 and 6 around it, and
a 1-D-first switch in the two campaign controllers. Every trajectory
differs from t's: the seed is inverted through the open paste, and the
bulk pH the soil biology reads is the open reading. Section 0u is the
whole procedure for this root. Sections 0t and 0 to 7 are the records of t
and o; they still describe the machinery, with alpha 3.4 wherever they say
1.3 and combination `a3p4_kn0p0048_kbfree_fd1` (cases 193 to 195) wherever
they name the campaign's sweep cases.
